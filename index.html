Monuments of India

Taj Mahal, Agra

  • Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh
  • Built: 1632-1653; about 390 years
  • Built for Mughal Empire (by Shah Jahan)

Red Fort, Delhi

  • Location: New Delhi
  • Built: 12 May 1639-6 April 1648; 378 years
  • Built for Mughal Empire

Gateway of India, Mumbai

  • Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Built: 1913-1924; about 102 years
  • Built for the visit of King George V

Qutub Minar, Delhi

  • Location: Mehrauli, New Delhi
  • Built: 1199-1220; over 800 years
  • Built for Delhi Sultanate

Lotus Temple, Delhi

  • Location: Bahapur, New Delhi
  • Built: 1976-1986; 40 years
  • Built as a Bahai House of Worship

Stone Chariot, Hampi

  • Location: Hampi, Karnataka
  • Built: 15th-16th century; about 500 years
  • Built for Vijayanagara Empire
